AutoStreamer

A small Windows app for people who stream the games they were going to play anyway.

It notices when you start a game and gets your stream ready for it: the right title and category on Twitch and YouTube, and OBS pointed at the game. It goes live only when you say so, and only you ever end the stream.

What it will not do

  • Put anything on air except the game.
  • Touch the game itself, or appear over it.
  • Send anything to us. There is no account and no telemetry.

AutoStreamer is in development and not yet available.
