AutoStreamer

A small Windows app for people who stream the games they were going to play anyway.

It sits in the tray, notices when a game starts, and asks whether you want to go live. If you say yes, it writes the stream title, category and tags for that game, starts OBS, and posts the announcement once the stream is actually up. When you quit the game, it stops. You do not open a settings screen on the way in or the way out.

What it does

Notices the game itself
No timers, no polling, no scanning your running processes. It hooks the same Windows events the taskbar uses, so it costs nothing while you are not playing.
Writes the metadata for you
Title from a template you control, the right category on each platform, and your tags. The thing that actually loses you viewers is a stream called "Untitled" in the wrong category, and that is the thing this removes.
Twitch and YouTube together
Both are first class. Connect either, or both.
Announces once you are really live
Never before, so nobody clicks through to an offline channel.
Stops when you stop
The channel does not sit there advertising a game you finished hours ago.

What it does not do

  • It does not touch the game process beyond asking Windows for its name. Nothing is injected, nothing is hooked inside it, and anti-cheat has nothing to object to.
  • It never steals focus. No window of ours will appear over a fullscreen game.
  • It does not ask for your stream key, your chat, or your revenue data. It requests the narrowest permission that does the job on each platform.
  • It sends nothing to us. There is no account, no server and no telemetry. With nothing connected, it makes no network requests at all.
